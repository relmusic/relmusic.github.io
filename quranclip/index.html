Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. Is Relmusic QuranClip completely free to use?

Yes! All core video editing, recording, and media downloading features are 100% free with no hidden subscription fees.

2. Can I upload my own custom background videos or images?

Absolutely. You can easily drag and drop your own MP4 files, GIFs, or high-resolution photos into the workspace upload box.

3. How do I select a specific verse to render?

Simply choose your preferred Surah from the dropdown menu, and select your target verse using the specific ayah number picker.

4. Are there multiple reciters available to choose from?

Yes, we provide 17 authentic Qaris featuring different styles including Murattal and Mujawwad recitations.

5. How does the live audio recording and saving work?

You can control audio playback directly in the preview workspace, click 'Rekam Clip' to capture your stream, and instantly download your creations from the history section.

6. What video formats are supported for export?

The tool supports standard web-compatible formats like WebM and MP4 for seamless social media sharing.

7. Can I choose different translations for the verses?

Yes, multiple translation languages are supported, including English (Saheeh International), Bahasa Indonesia, French, Spanish, and more.
