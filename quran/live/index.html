2. Does the tool include all 114 Surahs of the Holy Qur'an?
Yes, the Surah selector dropdown contains all 114 Surahs complete with their respective English names and total ayah counts from Al-Fatihah to An-Nas.
3. How do I change the background video during live streaming?
Simply select a different MP4 file from the video source dropdown menu (e.g., swiss.mp4 or italia.mp4), and the workspace canvas will update instantly.
4. Can I record my stream locally while broadcasting live?
Yes, clicking the "Record Video" button activates the browser's media recorder, allowing you to save an MP4 copy directly to your device after the session.
5. How do I connect my YouTube or Facebook live stream?
Copy your RTMP Server URL and Stream Key from your streaming dashboard (such as YouTube Studio), paste them into the RTMP configuration boxes, and click connect.
6. Where are my download history files saved?
Downloads are saved directly through your web browser's default download manager, and selected history items can be exported using the history download button.
