1. Understanding the Anatomy of IPv4 and IPv6 Addresses

At the foundational core of digital communications lie IPv4 and IPv6 protocols. IPv4 utilizes a 32-bit address space, providing roughly 4.29 billion distinct addresses, structured as four decimal octets separated by dots. Because of global exhaustion, IPv6 was introduced utilizing 128-bit addresses structured in hexadecimal blocks.
